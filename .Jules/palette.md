## 2025-04-28 - Contact Form Accessibility & Validation
**Learning:** Converting a 'div' based form container to a semantic '<form>' element with 'onsubmit="event.preventDefault(); submitForm(this);"' and proper '<label for="id">' associations significantly improves accessibility and enables native browser features like 'Enter to submit' and validation.
**Action:** Always prefer semantic '<form>' elements over 'div' containers for interactive data entry areas to leverage built-in browser UX.

## 2026-07-27 - Semantic Contact Form Accessibility and Async Loading State
**Learning:** Forms constructed out of `<div>` tags without `<form>` containers or correct `for`/`id` labels violate fundamental accessibility standards (WCAG) and prevent standard browser behaviors (such as submitting on Enter or native validation). Refactoring to a semantic `<form>` while introducing immediate visual feedback (disabling the button and changing text to "Enviando...") significantly enhances both assistive technology compatibility and the overall user experience by providing clear, secure confirmation of action progress.
**Action:** Always refactor custom div-based forms to standard semantic `<form>` tags, explicitly link all input controls to their respective `<label>` elements, and provide intuitive, disabled loading states during asynchronous submission flows.

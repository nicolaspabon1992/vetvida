## 2026-06-21 - Semantic Form Refactoring & Loading States
**Learning:** Refactoring div-based forms to semantic <form> elements with native validation (required, type) and providing immediate feedback via a simulated loading state (disabling button, changing text) significantly improves accessibility and perceived performance.
**Action:** Always prioritize semantic <form> elements and include basic loading states for any user-triggered actions to meet accessibility standards and provide clear UX feedback.

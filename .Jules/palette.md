## 2026-04-23 - Semantic Form for Native UX
**Learning:** Using a semantic `<form>` element instead of generic `<div>` containers unlocks critical native browser behaviors like "Enter to submit" and built-in validation messages without extra JavaScript. Properly associating labels with `id` and `for` is the foundation of form accessibility.
**Action:** Always refactor non-semantic form structures into standard HTML forms to provide a more robust and accessible experience for all users.

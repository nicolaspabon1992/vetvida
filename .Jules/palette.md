## 2025-05-14 - Accessibility & Scope Management
**Learning:** Bundling multiple accessibility fixes (navigation, forms, contact links) into a single PR can easily exceed the 50-line micro-UX constraint and violates the 'ONE improvement' rule. While well-intentioned, it makes the PR harder to review and deviates from the 'micro' philosophy.
**Action:** Prioritize the highest-impact UX/a11y issue (like keyboard navigation) and implement it solo to stay within line limits and maintain focus.

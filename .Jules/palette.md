
## 2026-06-30 - Semantic Form Refactoring for Accessibility and Feedback
**Learning:** Converting div-based forms to semantic <form> elements with native validation and explicit labels significantly improves accessibility (screen readers, keyboard navigation) and allows for robust loading states.
**Action:** Always use <form> elements, 'for/id' label associations, and 'onsubmit' handlers to manage asynchronous feedback and form resets.

## 2026-06-27 - Semantic Forms & Actionable Contacts
**Learning:** Refactoring <div>-based forms to semantic <form> elements with native validation attributes (required, type) and simulated loading states significantly improves both accessibility and user feedback without adding new dependencies.
**Action:** Always prioritize semantic <form> elements and ensure interactive buttons use functional links (like tel: or mailto:) where appropriate to provide immediate utility.

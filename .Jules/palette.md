# Palette's Journal - Critical Learnings Only

## 2026-05-28 - Actionable Emergency Contacts
**Learning:** In high-stress situations (like pet emergencies), every second counts. Non-functional contact information (like plain text phone numbers or dead buttons) increases cognitive load and user frustration. Actionable 'tel:' links provide immediate utility and reduce friction.
**Action:** Always ensure emergency contact numbers and associated buttons are implemented as functional links with 'tel:' URIs and clear ARIA labels.
